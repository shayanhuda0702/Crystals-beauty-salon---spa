# Crystal's Beauty Salon & Spa Inc.

A responsive, original salon/spa website built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Inspired by the broad section layout of Pro Beauty Academy, with original branding and copy.

## Open
Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the VS Code Live Server extension.

## Files
- `index.html` — page content and structure
- `styles.css` — layout, colors and responsive design
- `script.js` — mobile navigation, service filtering, and appointment inquiry interface

## Before publishing
- Confirm the business address, email, and operating hours before publishing.
- Replace fictional brand story with approved business information.
- Connect the appointment inquiry form to a secure booking service before accepting submissions online. Currently, the form directs visitors to contact the business by email and does not send or store inquiries.
- Confirm the services offered and any required licenses.
- The page loads third-party Unsplash photos and Google Fonts over the internet; review image licensing and replace with business-owned photography when possible.
